What was checked

This is a development example from a previously used game. Review is by the assistant, not independent human ground truth. The map does not establish miles run, speed or total time on floor. For a held ball, the diagram uses its identified carrier’s mapped feet when available. Otherwise it projects the camera position onto the floor and labels it ≈ when height is unknown or above the floor. This can be displaced from the true overhead location. Missing camera alignment or projections outside the rink remain unmapped. The CSV includes diagram coordinates and the projection method.
