How this replay was made

SAM2 tracks start from the ball-hockey detector. A player filter runs before association, and clear uniform contradictions can split a track. A pose model estimates the feet. Manually marked floor lines and camera registration place accepted estimates on a relative rink. No position is invented across an occlusion or camera failure.

The drawing is not a surveyed rink. Position errors and identity switches remain possible. It cannot establish miles run, speed, possession or total time on floor. White-shirt tracks are blue on the drawing; red-shirt tracks are red.

Earlier versions exposed goalie/field-player and opposing-uniform swaps. The added checks reduce some errors but also split correct tracks. Same-uniform identity swaps remain possible.
