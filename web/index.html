Data and limitations

Only NBHL ball-hockey footage is used. Scoring records are separate from model predictions. Game clocks show remaining time in ten-minute periods; scores show home – guest.

There are 24 reviewed goal clips: seven from California, seven from Massachusetts and ten from New Jersey. Video intervals are estimates from the play and reactions; a visible ball crossing is not established for every goal. The New Jersey recording ends before the clock reaches zero; its final score comes from the official record. Unknown statistics stay empty in the downloads.

The player detector was trained on ball-hockey footage and tested on separate recordings. Persistent identity, ball tracking and jersey recognition remain experimental. The audits use assistant-reviewed observations, not independent human ground truth.
