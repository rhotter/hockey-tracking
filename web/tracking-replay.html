About the image-model comparison

Both methods start with identical observed boxes and SAM2 fragments. The joint-image method blends Qwen3-VL Embedding 2B image vectors with the existing appearance scores before global linking. On this five-minute clip it recovered 21 of 26 reviewed same-player connections, versus 18 for the baseline, and removed both reviewed wrong joins. Across the four short clips it kept all 90 correct connections and zero reviewed wrong joins, but added two unverified joins.

The joint-image configuration was one of six challengers frozen before regression scoring. It tied the best development score; the preset tie-break selected the averaged-image configuration instead. This footage had been reviewed previously, and these sparse checks do not establish reliable full-game player identity. All image-model results and evidence.

About this SAM tracking method

Official pretrained SAM3 or SAM 3.1 Object Multiplex, native video detection and tracking, using the development-selected “person” prompt. The boxes come from model-emitted segmentation masks. They can include officials or spectators and may persist behind occlusion; they are not verified visibility or jersey identities. No positions are interpolated and no manual corrections are applied. All four fixed regression clips are available. Measured results, development variants and limitations.
