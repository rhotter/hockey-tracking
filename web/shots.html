How these counts work

A counted SOG is an attacking shot that scores, or is on target and stopped by the goalkeeper. Misses, skater blocks and post-only attempts do not count. A distinct rebound shot can count separately.

These are experimental automatic counts for the displayed segments. Unclear attempts stay in “Needs review.” The detector scans chronological video frames, then re-examines proposed plays at a denser sampling rate. It uses the surrounding play and visible ball evidence. Goalkeeper movement alone is insufficient.

Times refer to the clip and original recording, not the game clock. Player numbers are model readings when available, not verified player identities. No independently annotated full-game accuracy score is available.
